CONTINUUM MOSAIC · RHYTHM ENGINE

40-track sample + synthesis rhythm workstation · 16/32/64 steps · one hit per step

01 · Master Transport

STEP 01

Activate audio, then Generate Demo Samples or load your own one-shots.

03 · Rhythm Sequencer · 40 Tracks

Tap a step to select it for subdivision editing. The tap also toggles its straight hit; configure positions in the editor below. Amber = active. Each track has one hit per step.

SUBDIVISION WORKSTATION · Selected Track & Step

Select a track and step with EDIT in the grid. Divide the chosen step or the containing beat into 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 or 12 independently triggered positions. Beat subdivisions are mapped onto the shared four-step beat timeline.

Track 01 · Step 01
Each sequencer step supports 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 or 12 divisions. Beat mode schedules positions across the four steps of its containing beat. Click a microstep to toggle it; select it to change velocity. 16/32/64 are the number of main steps, not the number of tracks.

40-Track Pattern Grid · Click to Toggle

Multiple independently timed hits per step. The window displays 16 of 40 tracks. Scroll vertically for other tracks and horizontally for 32/64 steps.

02A · CONTINUUM SAMPLE LIBRARY

Browse, audition and load samples into the existing 40 tracks.

Press CONNECT / REFRESH to browse the Sample Library.

Kit Voice Loader · Combine Multiple Banks

Choose a source bank here (or from the Sound Sources library), then add its individual voices to a chosen empty track, the next empty track, or append an entire bank without erasing other tracks. LOAD KIT replaces all tracks deliberately; these ADD buttons do not. Each track has independent Voice Designer settings, sample loading, sequencer steps and mixer controls.

02 · Sound Sources · 40 Tracks

ONE SHOT = sequenced hits · SYNC LOOP = bar-aligned loop, tempo-matched by playback speed (pitch changes) · FREE PLAY = independent audition. Set the source loop length in bars. STOP halts synchronised loops.

Kit selections are original synthesis interpretations, not recordings of the named hardware. Upload a file directly beside any track name.

Selected Track · Voice Designer

Loading a voice into a destination track automatically selects it here. Click a Sound Sources track name or PLAY to select it for individual editing. Changes affect that track's audition and sequencer playback.

04 · Live Instrument Trigger Pads

Looper idle · AUTO PHRASE waits at least the selected minimum bars; FINISH PHRASE closes at the next bar boundary. FIXED BARS records the full selected length.

Pads trigger all 40 tracks. Optional keyboard triggers: 1–0 for tracks 1–10, Q–P for tracks 11–20 (when not typing).

05 · Track Mixer · Vertical Channels

Each track has a visibly expanded TRACK FX section with cutoff, resonance, saturation and sample attack/decay. Effects are saved with projects and remain separate from the Shared Effects Rack.

06 · Shared Effects Rack

Compact vertical effects strips. Each has an independent ON switch and wet-level fader; all effects are bypassed by default. Bitcrusher uses a waveshaper approximation.

SHARED DRUM SCULPTOR · FILTER & ENVELOPE

Global filter shapes the combined drum output. Sample envelope applies to each newly triggered sample, without changing the original audio file.

07 · Integration Notes

Standalone prototype. Sample and/or three-layer synthesised voice → per-track filter / resonance / drive → track gain / pan → dry master and optional effect sends → master output. Sequencer timing is kept separate from audio routing for later Continuum Mosaic integration. Saved projects contain voice assignments, voice-design parameters, patterns and settings, but not uploaded audio files: reload your samples after reopening. No changes to the main Mosaic beta.
